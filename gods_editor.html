GODS

Level editor for the DOS version (Bitmap Brothers, 1992)

Open the folder that holds GAME.EXE and the PLEV*.MAP files. The editor reads the maps, tiles, sprites and the PALFILS files that carry every trigger, monster and mechanism.

  • Nothing leaves your computer.
  • In Chrome or Edge, Save level writes straight into the folder and keeps the original files in EDITOR_BACKUP.
  • In other browsers the changed files are downloaded instead.
